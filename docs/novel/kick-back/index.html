Synopsis

Beyond the Wall, monstrous beings known as Contaminants roamed freely.

One day, people began turning into monsters without reason.

After a sudden terrorist attack, those who wanted to kill the president’s daughter started to close in.

Just when death was inevitable, a man appeared to save her.

“Nice to meet you. Starting today, I’ll be assigned to protect you…”

He hesitated, as if unsure how to define himself, one brow arching before a faint smirk curved his lips.

“You could say I’m just a mutt who’s been surviving on the streets.”

***

“W-wait, hold on! Are you really going to… already?”

“Yeah, I’m in a bit of a hurry.”

Do Sagyeom pressed Yoo Ian down by the nape until her upper body was bent over the vanity.
His answer came lazy and unbothered as he adjusted his belt.

“Does it… hurt a lot?”

“I don’t like it when people make too much of a fuss, but…”

“Uh…”

“I’m not the kind of guy who doesn’t know what he’s doing either.”

He gave a quiet chuckle at her startled expression, clearly amused by her reaction.

“This feels weird…”

“When it’s like this, you’re supposed to say it feels good.”

His movements stayed deliberate, his tone teasing. Ian trembled under the unfamiliar closeness but couldn’t pull away.

“Do you think your father, the president, would know? That his perfectly raised daughter is letting some street rat touch her like this?”

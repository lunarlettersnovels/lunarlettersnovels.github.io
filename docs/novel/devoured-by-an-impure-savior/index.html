Synopsis

“You’ve been having an affair in your dream, wife. I didn’t know my wife could be so vulgar.”

Her husband, who had been kind to her throughout their marriage, suddenly changed after she dreamed of a demon.

Nieve was treated like an animal. Her life was as fragile as a candle flame that could be extinguished at any moment.

“I wouldn’t have allowed my wife to take care of another man.”

“So, keep worrying and sympathizing with me as you do now, Nieve.”

Then, in the midst of her difficult life, Delphion appeared like a savior.

Nieve helplessly fell for the mysterious and languid atmosphere surrounding him.

“When will you remember me, my dear?”

As she continued her illicit affair with the demon, her body and mind became more consumed by desire…

“You shouldn’t have given me your heart, Nieve.”

The moment she discovered her husband’s shocking secret, who she thought was merely a womanizer—a storm swept into her previously peaceful life.

“Do you call the one you love your savior?”

To survive, she had to take the savior’s hand, no matter what comes ahead.

“But what can we do? We’ve already done so much, even in our dreams. You and I.”

Even if it’s impure.

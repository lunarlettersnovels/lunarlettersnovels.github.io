On the other side, Fang Qiuye, who really was experiencing serious hair loss, was in pain and happiness.

She had already signed a contract with the program team. As soon as she met with them, she was told not to be afraid of Yaoyao’s pet snakes. She was also warned that she shouldn’t touch them at will. And since Yaoyao had Little Sweetheart at home, she should be disinfected before she went in…

Hearing these made her want to take a look at Little Sweetheart more! She couldn’t even refuse!

In the end, Fang Qiuye admitted that she didn’t have very good English skills. However, the staff smiled and said that it didn’t matter. As long as she knew basic grammar, it was fine. They also recommended a translation app to her.

This program group was simply not serious about helping Yaoyao to level up her English skill. But Fang Qiuye didn’t want to be lax. So, she decided to work hard and not fool her idol.

Therefore, she mustered courage to meet her blind date and held dates with him to learn English for half an hour as they ate. Her rental unit also happened to be not far from the university where this teacher worked. She did a lot of homework and asked a lot of questions when she met the teacher. When she arrived at the restaurant, she only ate a mouthful of her favorite meal, and she stopped touching the dishes after that.

She also asked her question respectfully, “Teacher Gong, can you please teach me how to translate this sentence and its grammar?”

She collected all the praises of Yaoyao from her foreign fans on the Internet. After looking them up using the dictionary and Baidu translation, she roughly knew what they meant, but it was not enough! Was this an object clause or an adverbial or attributive clause? She must understand and teach Yaoyao the principles!

Fang Qiuye was dead serious.

Gong Xuechang wore a clean-looking shirt, but when he saw the gaunt girl with dark circles under the eyes, wearing black-rimmed glasses, and casual sportswear, he was shocked.

“Translate?” he asked.

Fang Qiuye nodded. “Yup!”

“Domineering boss… one night… times…?”

Gong Xuechang was speechless! Fang Qiuye was shocked as him, and she almost knocked over the condiment bottles beside her.

This was the cover she had commissioned for her new book. D*mn it! She accidentally put it together with the learning materials!

“D*mn, I accidentally opened this! No, I turned the wrong page!” she hastily said and hid the material. “Sorry, this is a misunderstanding!”

Fang Qiuye felt she would choke to death!

*

After nearly two weeks of studying hard, Fang Qiuye finally welcomed the fateful day where she would meet her idol.b

After struggling for two hours, she finally chose a pink skirt that gave youthful vibes. She also put on a light peach blossom makeup and wore a pair of high heels. At once, the appearance of the haggard coder was turned into a cute girl.

When she went out and got into the car, there was already a cameraman following her. This kind of treatment and experience almost made Fang Qiuye trip.

It turned out that Yaoyao lived a life where she didn’t get any privacy! In front of the camera, even an unsightly yawn would be filmed. The pressure she felt was extremely high.

The netizens in the live broadcast room quickly discovered that this time, there was also a combination of a large and a small image in the live broadcast. On one side, Yaoyao was cleaning up the house with Mr. Shen and Little Bun to welcome their guests. On the other side, it was Fang Qiuye who was heading out cautiously.

YY fans laughed upon seeing this!

[Yaoyao looks like she’s really expecting guests~ She’s cleaning up so carefully~]

[She does look like the hostess!]

[Wait, is this our president? Haha, her look is so cute and childish!]

[Cheers to the president!]

[The president’s backpack is the same as mine. Is she carrying the learning materials? It looks so heavy!]

As the show had once again released the broadcast time in advance, many people were watching it. The fans were all waiting excitedly. To them, the scene where Yaoyao welcomes the president was worth the wait. After all, the president represented all YY fans. Watching the president head to Yaoyao’s house and talking to her would make them feel like they had experienced it for themselves. They were all very excited!

Gong Xuechang, who didn’t have to teach today, also set an alarm clock beforehand. He clicked on the link sent to him by his blind date yesterday. She asked him to check it out, and she said she would also buy him dinner at a later time.

Little did he expect, he saw two women in two different images as soon as he entered the room. His eyelids jumped on the spot. The curly-haired girl in a pink dress and delicate makeup looked a little familiar.

Gong Xuechang took a deep breath… This was his raunchy blind date!?

After having meals together for nearly ten days, his blind date who always came to meet him with messy hair, baggy clothes and sneakers was now all dressed up wearing makeup!?

He couldn’t help but glance at the title of the live broadcast. ‘Fan meeting her idol to teach her English.’

She was dressed vastly different and better now compared to when she was meeting him for the blind date! However, what caught Gong Xuechang’s eyes were something different.

‘Hold on, to teach English!?’

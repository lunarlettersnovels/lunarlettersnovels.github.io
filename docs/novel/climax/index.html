Synopsis

In a second-story house in an unfamiliar city, Yeon-ha found herself living with a landlord named Kwon Min-gyu, who had a rough appearance, unrefined speech, and an intimidating presence.

“Let’s live together until your ankle heals.”

One day, Min-gyu suddenly proposed to Yeon-ha, who had lost her only guardian. Cornered, she took his hand and thus began their seemingly peaceful yet precarious cohabitation.

“I told you, you don’t have to do anything. Just focus on getting your ankle healed.”

“Whenever you want to leave, just tell me. I’ll let you go cleanly.”

Although his affection seemed rough, it was pure, and Yeon-ha gradually opened her heart to him, finding herself unknowingly drawn to him.

But cruel reality began to tear them apart…

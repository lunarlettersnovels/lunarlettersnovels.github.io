About Us

Lunar Letters is a home for readers who love losing themselves in a good story.

We built Lunar Letters to make reading novels online simple, clean, and enjoyable. No clutter, no distractions — just you and the words on the page. Our library brings together a growing collection of titles across genres so you can always find your next read.

Our Mission

We believe great stories deserve a great reading experience. That's why we focus on fast, distraction-free pages that work beautifully on any device, day or night. Whether you're catching up on an ongoing series or discovering something new, Lunar Letters is designed to get out of your way and let the story shine.

What We Offer

  • A curated, ever-growing library of novels.
  • A clean, mobile-friendly reader with light and dark modes.
  • Easy tracking of the series you're following.

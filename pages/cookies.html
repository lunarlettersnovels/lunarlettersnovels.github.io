Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Lunar Letters uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites function properly, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. We also use your browser's local storage, which works similarly to remember settings on your device.

How We Use Them

Type Purpose
Essential Remember preferences such as your light/dark theme and reading progress.
Analytics Help us understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
Advertising Some pages may serve ads through third-party networks that can set their own cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

Third parties such as advertising or font providers may set cookies when their content loads on our pages. We do not control these cookies; please refer to the relevant provider's privacy policy for details.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings, and clear local storage at any time. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work, such as remembering your theme preference.

More Information

For more on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy, or contact us with any questions.
